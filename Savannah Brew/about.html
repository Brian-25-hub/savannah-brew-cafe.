Our Story

Savanna Brew Cafe opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee.

Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today the cafe supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month.

Our Values

We use freshly roasted Kenyan coffee beans.

Community

We provide a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

We use compostable cups and reduce waste.
